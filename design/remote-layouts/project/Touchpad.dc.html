What each part is called

1

App switcher

Top bar: Laptop · YouTube · Prime · Netflix · Jellyfin. Picking one also switches Chrome's tab.

2

Title bar (title card)

Status and time ("Watching · Playing · 0:41 / 2:09"), then the title and channel of what's playing, or of the highlighted tile.

3

Search button

Opens the search box inside the title bar itself. No separate search bar.

4

Progress edge

The title bar's bottom border doubles as the progress line. Display only.

6

Swipe pad

Swipe to move (long swipe = several steps), tap the middle for OK, tap an edge for one step, hold an edge to repeat.

7

Button grid · navigation row

Back · Home · Focus · Mini · Reload

8

Button grid · volume & video row

YT − · YT + · CC · Speed · Full

9

Button grid · extras row

Prev · Theater · PC Mute · TV · Next

10

Playback bar (dock)

Fixed at the bottom: PC − · −10 · Play/Pause · +10 · PC +

5 (results toggle) and the results sheet are on the next board.
